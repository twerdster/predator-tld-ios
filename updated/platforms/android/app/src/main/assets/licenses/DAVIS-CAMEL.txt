DAVIS camel recording (first 90 frames)

Dataset: https://davischallenge.org/
MP4 mirror: https://huggingface.co/datasets/emirkisa/DAVIS-2017-480p-mp4
File: camel_raw_24fps.mp4

DAVIS homepage dataset metadata and the mirror dataset card specify

Dataset authors: Jordi Pont-Tuset, Federico Perazzi, Sergi Caelles,
Pablo Arbelaez, Alexander Sorkine-Hornung and Luc Van Gool.

This Android asset is the first 90 decoded frames, resized to 320x180 and
converted to unannotated 8-bit grayscale pixels. The application computes
tracking boxes and path overlays at runtime. Initial region is manually
specified as x=105,y=35,width=135,height=85 in the resized first frame.
No DAVIS masks or later-frame ground truth are supplied to the tracker.
Recorded boxes are qualitative rather than an accuracy evaluation.

Source download and source/derived hashes are recorded separately in the
repository's updated/DATA_PROVENANCE.json and platform validation record.
Footage terms remain separate from recovered source licensing.
